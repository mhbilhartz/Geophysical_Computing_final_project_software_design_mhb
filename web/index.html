How to interpret this model

This is a 2.5D DC forward model: conductivity is invariant along an infinitely long tunnel, while point-source current spreads in three dimensions. The finite-difference solver uses harmonic conductivity across cell faces.

The surface is insulating away from the electrodes. Finite side/bottom walls use the analytical homogeneous primary potential. The tunnel anomaly uses the corresponding secondary field. Grid, boundary and Fourier sensitivity still matter; a small residual does not certify continuum accuracy.

The Onyx mine in Steamboat Springs motivates this teaching project. No measured mine data, instrument noise, contact resistance, topography or inversion is included. Apparent resistivity is not a reconstructed depth image. Water and air presets are illustrative.
